Every message crosses the solar system through two relays, can be lost on the way, and arrives hours late from the outer planets. How a trade works

You send copies.
Your order leaves as several independent copies, enough that one arrives on time 99.5% of the time.
A light-cone deadline.
Each batch accepts your order only if it could have left before the cutoff, so nobody trades on news that hadn't reached them.
One fair price.
The batch clears at Earth at a single price for everyone in it.
Exactly once.
Money is held at the source, credited once at the destination, and only the destination can say it never arrived.

The relays share one orbit, 90° apart, so the line between them always passes 2 AU from the Sun, and the Sun can block at most a narrow cone around each planet. One relay is always open, from every settlement, at every moment.

Risk and capital

A default is only discovered when a margin call goes unanswered, and from Neptune that takes hours. MPX sizes margin for the slowest round trip, adds fat-tail protection, and keeps every default resource at the hub.

Margin calculator

Margin period = worst round trip to Earth + 1 h grace + 1.5 h to close out. Initial margin = 2.62 × volatility × √(period) × position, where 2.62 covers 99% of fat-tailed (Student-t) price moves.

If a member defaults

  1. Its own initial margin
  2. Its unpaid variation margin
  3. Its share of the default fund
  4. MPX's own capital (25% of the fund)
  5. Other members' fund shares, sized to survive the two largest defaults at a 6-sigma move
  6. Assessments, prefunded at the hub for outer planets
  7. Recovery: haircuts on gains, then partial tear-up
Members also hold liquid resources of 30% of notional. An isolated member is hedged, not defaulted.
